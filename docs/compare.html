How this is measured

Method

Both sources run the same site: query per alert. The alerts are read every 20 minutes; Serper searches every 60 minutes over the past week (the past day for Bloomberg Law, SAP Financial Management), paging 10 results at a time. Serper's first search for each feed (Oct 9, 14:37 UTC) is a baseline: it isn't counted as found, but it does count as Serper having the page if the alert reports it later. Pages are matched by URL, ignoring tracking parameters; listing and archive pages (/news, /page/3, /tag/...) are left out on both sides. A page first found in the last 12 hours is still settling, because the other source may not have checked yet; the headline leaves those out. Before Serper started, the alerts had already found 43 pages; 11 of them are in Serper's records. Serper has run 21 times and used 995 credits.
